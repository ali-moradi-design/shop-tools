# Demo product photos

These 500 product photos come from the public product pages of www.dewalt.ae
(see `manifest.json` for the page and source URL of each file). They were
downscaled to 900 px WebP.

- All photos and brand names are the property of their owners (DEWALT /
  Stanley Black & Decker). They are **not** covered by this repository's license.
- They are used only as placeholder content in a non-commercial portfolio demo.
  The payment gateway is simulated and nothing is sold.
- If an owner asks for removal, delete this folder; the shop shows a placeholder
  for products without a photo.
